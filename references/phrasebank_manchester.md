## 3. RINGKASAN FRASA KUNCI FREKUENSI TINGGI

### 3.1 Menyatakan Kesenjangan Riset (*Research Gap*)
- *"However, these approaches have predominantly focused on X, while paying little attention to Y."*
- *"Despite recent advances in [field], limited research has addressed the challenge of..."*
- *"Previous studies have established that X occurs; however, the precise mechanism underlying Y remains poorly understood."*

### 3.2 Menjelaskan Batasan Penelitian (*Acknowledging Limitations*)
- *"A potential limitation of this study is the relatively small sample size, which may constrain generalizability."*
- *"Due to hardware constraints on edge microcontrollers, the model was restricted to..."*
- *"These findings should be interpreted with caution given the preliminary nature of the dataset."*

### 3.3 Menyatakan Rekomendasi Riset Lanjutan (*Future Work*)
- *"Further research is warranted to validate these results under real-world acoustic interference."*
- *"Future investigations will explore the integration of multi-spectral sensor fusion to enhance robustness."*
- *"It would be beneficial to extend this framework to larger, heterogeneous environments."*
