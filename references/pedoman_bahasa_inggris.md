## 1. PRINSIP DASAR: INTERNATIONAL SCHOLARLY REGISTER

Bahasa Inggris akademik formal (*scholarly English*) memiliki karakteristik baku:
1. **Objektivitas & Larangan Pronomina Personal:**  
   Hindari kata ganti orang pertama (*I, We, Our, Us*). Gunakan kalimat pasif orang ketiga (*third-person passive*) atau jadikan artifak/studi sebagai subjek aktif (*"This study evaluates...", "A prototype was deployed..."*).
2. **Kepadatan Leksikal (*High Lexical Density*):**  
   Lebih menyukai pemadatan frase nomina (*nominalization*) daripada rangkaian klausa panjang.
3. **Bahasa Berhati-Hati (*Hedging / Cautious Language*):**  
   Hindari klaim mutlak seperti *"This proves that..."*; gunakan bahasa terukur seperti *"These findings suggest that..."* atau *"The data indicates a plausible correlation..."*.
4. **Presisi Teknis & Konsistensi Terminologi:**  
   Satu konsep menggunakan satu istilah baku dari awal sampai akhir naskah.

## 2. DAFTAR HITAM KOSAKATA KLISE AI (*AI VOCABULARY BLACKLIST*)

Berikut adalah kata-kata dan pola frasa yang paling sering diwaspadai oleh editor dan reviewer jurnal internasional sebagai indikator teks buatan mesin:

### 2.1 Kosakata Klise AI yang Wajib Dieliminasi
| Kosakata Klise AI | Masalah Gaya | Pengganti Akademik Formal yang Tepat |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Delve / Delve into** | Klise terbesar AI; dramatis | **Examine, investigate, analyze, explore** |
| **Crucial / Pivotal** | Berlebihan; hiperbolis | **Important, essential, significant, critical** |
| **Notably / Underscore** | Penegas mekanis AI | **Significantly, specifically, highlight, demonstrate** |
| **Landscape** (*in the AI landscape*) | Metafora jurnalistik usang | **Domain, field, context, ecosystem, state-of-the-art** |
| **Leverage** (*leverage this method*) | Jargon bisnis korporat | **Utilize, apply, employ, exploit, adopt** |
| **Realm / Tapestry / Beacon** | Bahasa puitis non-ilmiah | **Area, domain, spectrum, framework** |
| **Multifaceted / Testament** | Diksi dramatis AI | **Complex, comprehensive, evidence, indication** |
| **Foster / Harness** | Metafora persuasif | **Promote, enable, improve, capture, utilize** |
| **Seamless / Cutting-edge** | Gaya bahasa brosur pemasaran | **Integrated, automated, modern, state-of-the-art** |
| **Game-changer / Paradigm shift** | Klaim berlebihan tanpa bukti | **Substantial advancement, notable improvement** |

### 2.2 Pola Kalimat Retorika Mesin yang Dilarang
1. **Pola Kontras Paksa (*Forced Contrast*):**  
   Hindari pola berulang *"X, not Y"* atau *"X rather than Y"* dalam judul atau narasi utama.  
   *Hindari:* *"An adaptive system rather than a static timer."*  
   *Pakai:* *"An adaptive frequency-modulated system."*
2. **Kalimat Slogan Pendek (*Punchline Sentences*):**  
   Hindari kalimat filosofis pendek seperti *"The contrast is the paper."* atau *"Simplicity is efficiency."*. Jurnal ilmiah menuntut pelaporan faktual.
3. **Penggunaan Tanda Pisah Em-Dash (`—`) Berlebihan:**  
   Batasi em-dash maksimal 2–3 per 1.000 kata. Jangan jadikan em-dash sebagai jalan pintas menghindari struktur anak kalimat atau tanda koma/titik-dua.

## 3. KAIDAH TENSES, ANATOMI IMRAD, & PUNGTUASI MATEMATIS

### 3.1 Aturan Baku Tenses per Seksi Naskah
| Bagian Naskah | Tense Baku yang Wajib Digunakan | Contoh Kalimat Baku |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Introduction** | **Present Simple** (Fakta umum & teori mapan)<br>**Present Perfect** (Tren riset terkini)<br>**Past Simple** (Temuan peneliti terdahulu tertentu) | • *"Rats possess acute auditory sensitivity in the ultrasonic spectrum."*<br>• *"Recent studies have demonstrated the efficacy of edge computing..."*<br>• *"Hanson et al. (2015) identified habituation as a primary failure mode."* |
| **Methods** | **Past Simple (Passive Voice)** (Langkah prosedur eksperimen yang sudah selesai dilakukan) | • *"Audio signals were sampled at 44.1 kHz using an I2S microphone."*<br>• *"The model was trained for 50 epochs using binary cross-entropy loss."* |
| **Results** | **Past Simple** (Pelaporan temuan metrik kuantitatif terukur) | • *"The proposed architecture achieved a classification accuracy of 96.8%."*<br>• *"Latency decreased from 45 ms to 12 ms."* |
| **Discussion** | **Present Simple** (Interpretasi makna temuan)<br>**Cautious Modals** (Diskusi implikasi teoretis) | • *"These results indicate that frequency randomization suppresses habituation."*<br>• *"The performance discrepancy may be attributed to ambient acoustic noise."* |
| **Conclusions** | **Present Simple** (Sintesis capaian)<br>**Future Simple / Modals** (Arah riset masa depan) | • *"This paper presents a standalone offline rat repellent system."*<br>• *"Future work will incorporate multi-node mesh networking."* |

### 3.2 Kaidah Angka, Desimal, dan Simbol Ilmiah
1. **Tanda Pisah Rentang (*En-dash* `–`):**  
   Gunakan en-dash (`–`), bukan tanda hubung (`-`), untuk rentang angka, tahun, atau halaman:  
   *2016–2026*, *pp. 45–77*, *20–50 kHz*.
2. **Tanda Minus Matematis Asli (`−`):**  
   Gunakan karakter Unicode minus (`−` / U+2212), bukan tanda hubung keyboard (`-`), untuk nilai negatif:  
   *−12 dB*, *−40 °C*.
3. **Spasi Satuan Pengukuran:**  
   Berikan satu spasi antara angka dan satuan unit (*600 dpi*, *30 min*, *44.1 kHz*, *5 V*, *10 mA*).  
   **Pengecualian:** Persentase (`%`) dan derajat (`°`) ditulis rapat tanpa spasi (*96.8%*, *25°C*).
4. **Angka Desimal:**  
   Gunakan tanda titik (`.`) untuk desimal dalam bahasa Inggris (*0.85*, bukan *0,85*). Samakan jumlah digit di belakang koma untuk metrik yang sama di seluruh naskah (misal semua metrik akurasi 3 desimal: *0.968*).
5. **Angka Pembuka Kalimat:**  
   Angka di awal kalimat **wajib dieja dengan huruf**:  
   • *Salah:* "50 audio samples were recorded..."  
   • *Benar:* "Fifty audio samples were recorded..." atau "A total of 50 audio samples were recorded..."

## 4. MANCHESTER ACADEMIC PHRASEBANK RESMI (18 KATEGORI LENGKAP)

Berikut adalah kompilasi bank frasa ilmiah internasional baku untuk setiap manuver retorika naskah ilmiah:

### 4.1 Introducing Work (Membuka Karya Ilmiah & Menjelaskan Urgensi)
- **Menegaskan Signifikansi Bidang Riset:**
  - *"In recent years, there has been an increasing interest in..."*
  - *"Acoustic pest control plays a pivotal role in sustainable urban hygiene..."*
  - *"The development of lightweight edge-AI algorithms has become a central focus in..."*
- **Menyoroti Masalah Riil Lapangan:**
  - *"However, conventional ultrasonic repellents suffer from rapid rodent habituation."*
  - *"Despite these advancements, power consumption and latency remain substantial bottlenecks."*
  - *"A major challenge in deploying deep learning on low-power microcontrollers is..."*
- **Menyatakan Tujuan Penelitian:**
  - *"The primary objective of this study is to design and evaluate an autonomous..."*
  - *"This paper proposes a novel framework that integrates log-mel spectrogram analysis with..."*
  - *"To address these limitations, this research investigates the feasibility of..."*

### 4.2 Referring to Literature (Menyitir Studi Terdahulu & Research Gap)
- **Menyintesis Literatur:**
  - *"Previous studies have established that rats exhibit distress vocalizations in the range of..."*
  - *"A considerable body of literature has demonstrated the efficacy of CNNs in acoustic classification (Chen & Ran, 2019; Park et al., 2022)."*
  - *"Early work by Hanson et al. (2015) focused primarily on single-frequency deterrents."*
- **Menemukan Celah Riset (*Research Gap*):**
  - *"However, these studies were largely limited to cloud-connected architectures with high latency."*
  - *"Much of the current literature pays little attention to the offline execution on embedded MCUs."*
  - *"While log-mel representations are widely used, their optimization for 8-bit integer quantization has not been systematically investigated."*

### 4.3 Describing Methods (Menjelaskan Prosedur, Alat, & Algoritma)
- **Menjelaskan Perangkat Keras & Komponen:**
  - *"The experimental setup consisted of an ESP32-S3 microcontroller coupled with an I2S digital microphone."*
  - *"Audio data was acquired at a sampling rate of 44.1 kHz with 16-bit resolution."*
  - *"The device was housed in an IP65-rated enclosure to ensure moisture resistance."*
- **Menjelaskan Prosedur & Pra-pemrosesan:**
  - *"The raw audio stream was segmented into 1-second sliding windows with 50% overlap."*
  - *"A Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) with a window length of 1024 points was applied to compute..."*
  - *"The log-mel spectrograms were normalized to zero mean and unit variance prior to inference."*
- **Menjelaskan Arsitektur Model & Pelatihan:**
  - *"The neural network architecture comprises three convolutional blocks followed by a dense classification layer."*
  - *"The model was optimized using the Adam optimizer with an initial learning rate of 0.001."*
  - *"To prevent overfitting, spatial dropout (0.2) and batch normalization were incorporated."*

### 4.4 Reporting Results (Melaporkan Temuan Kuantitatif & Merujuk Visual)
- **Merujuk Tabel dan Gambar:**
  - *"As illustrated in Fig. 1, the hardware pipeline operates in three sequential phases."*
  - *"The quantitative performance metrics are summarized in Table 2."*
  - *"It can be observed from Fig. 4 that the loss function converged after 35 epochs."*
- **Melaporkan Nilai Metrik Statistik:**
  - *"The proposed system achieved an overall accuracy of 96.8% and an F1-score of 0.965."*
  - *"A significant reduction in false positives was observed (p < 0.001, t-test)."*
  - *"The model demonstrated a peak inference latency of 12.4 ms with a power draw of 85 mA."*

### 4.5 Discussing Findings (Membahas Makna Temuan & Implikasi)
- **Menghubungkan Temuan dengan Teori:**
  - *"These findings are consistent with those of Peffers et al. (2007), confirming that structured artifact evaluation..."*
  - *"This outcome supports the hypothesis that randomized ultrasonic sweep patterns suppress rodent habituation."*
  - *"The observed accuracy surge can be explained by the superior frequency resolution of the log-mel filterbank."*
- **Menjelaskan Anomali atau Hasil Tak Terduga:**
  - *"An unexpected finding was the slight latency increase during background fan noise bursts."*
  - *"This discrepancy is likely attributable to environmental acoustic reflections within the enclosed warehouse."*

### 4.6 Being Critical & Using Cautious Language (*Hedging*)
- **Bahasa Berhati-Hati (*Hedging*):**
  - *"The data suggests that embedded audio classification is feasible for edge pest deterrence."*
  - *"It is likely that ambient humidity influenced the effective ultrasonic propagation radius."*
  - *"These results appear to indicate a strong correlation between sweep velocity and deterrence rate."*
- **Kritik Terukur & Limitasi Riset:**
  - *"A potential limitation of this methodology is the relatively small sample size of field recordings."*
  - *"The generalizability of these findings may be constrained by the acoustic profile of the testing facility."*

### 4.7 Signalling Transitions (Transisi Antar-Paragraf Formal)
- **Menyatakan Konsekuensi:** *Consequently, As a consequence, Therefore, Hence, Accordingly.*
- **Menyatakan Kontras:** *Conversely, In contrast, On the other hand, Nonetheless, Nevertheless.*
- **Menambahkan Argumen:** *Furthermore, In addition, Moreover, Additionally.*
- **Menyatakan Contoh:** *For instance, To illustrate, Specifically, As exemplified by.*

### 4.8 Writing Conclusions (Menyusun Kesimpulan & Riset Masa Depan)
- **Menyimpulkan Temuan Utama:**
  - *"In conclusion, this study has demonstrated the feasibility of an offline edge-computing intelligent rat repellent system."*
  - *"The empirical evidence confirms that combining log-mel spectrograms with adaptive ultrasonic waveforms achieves superior deterrence."*
- **Rekomendasi Riset Lanjutan:**
  - *"Further research is needed to evaluate multi-node acoustic coordination across large agricultural fields."*
  - *"Future investigations should explore the integration of battery-harvesting solar modules."*

## 5. CHECKLIST FINAL SEBELUM SUBMISI ARTIKEL

Gunakan checklist deterministik ini (setara `check_manuscript_en.py`) sebelum naskah diserahkan ke reviewer:
- [ ] **0 Kata Klise AI**: Bersih dari kata *delve, crucial, notably, landscape, leverage, tapestry*.
- [ ] **0 Pronomina Personal**: Tidak ada kata *We, Our, I, Us*.
- [ ] **Kesesuaian Tenses**: Methods dalam *Past Passive*, Introduction dalam *Present/Present Perfect*, Results dalam *Past*.
- [ ] **Pungtuasi Angka**: Tanda pisah rentang menggunakan *en-dash* (`–`), minus menggunakan `−`, desimal menggunakan titik (`.`).
- [ ] **Seluruh Gambar dan Tabel Disebut di Teks**: Dipanggil berurutan (`Fig. 1` sebelum `Fig. 2`, `Table 1` sebelum `Table 2`).
- [ ] **Integritas Field Code Zotero**: Struktur `<w:fldChar>` dan `ADDIN ZOTERO_ITEM` utuh tanpa jejak kotor.
